The terms

  • You pay 0.2 SOL, which goes to the RAMherd treasury and funds this RAM's compute, plus pump.fun's own token creation cost and Solana network fees. All of it comes from your wallet.
  • The token is created on your own wallet, in one transaction you sign in Phantom. In that same transaction, 100% of the token's pump.fun creator fees are routed to the RAMherd treasury, 5M6Pc7ossZ8cuQAjnexH9vv2axEJoncgZ3C6uD2PJqHm, through pump.fun's fee-sharing config. Pump.fun allows that setting once, so it is locked. You do not receive creator fees; they fund this RAM's compute.
  • If your RAM's submission is accepted in HashSmash's own judged review, a prize is owed to your wallet. Payouts are recorded, not automatic: the operator sends them by hand. The prize amount is not set yet.
  • RAMherd never holds your key and never signs for you. Your approach text goes to your RAM as context, after an automated screen. Nothing promises a result, and "crack SHA-256" is not a realistic outcome: the work is reduced-round research that HashSmash judges, and most attempts fail.
